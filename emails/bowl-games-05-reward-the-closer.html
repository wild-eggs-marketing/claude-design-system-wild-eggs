BOWL GAMES  ·   LOCATIONS  ·   MY ACCOUNT

Short version: printed receipt, one card per qualifying receipt, link your Crazy Points account, rewards load the next day and expire 30 days after that.

Purchase required. Bowl Games runs September 20 – November 14, 2026 at all fifteen Crazy Bowls & Wraps locations in Missouri and Illinois. Play your own printed Crazy Bowls & Wraps receipt within 14 days of purchase; one play per order number. Your order decides which Huddle card a receipt earns, and not every receipt earns one. Rewards require a linked Crazy Points account, load the next day, expire 30 days after they load, and are limited to one of each per person per season; no cash value, not transferable. Collect all five Huddle cards and a regular bowl or wrap is on us; the set must be completed within the season. Taco Kit: weeks run Sunday through Saturday, through November 14, 2026. At each store, the guest with the most verified receipt scans that week wins a Taco Kit, dinner for four to five; ties are broken by the earliest qualifying scan. Winners are decided by scan count, not by random draw, and are notified by email; one kit per person for the season. Full rules: crazybowlsandwraps.com/bowl-games.
