BOWL GAMES  ·   LOCATIONS  ·   MY ACCOUNT

Short version: printed receipt, one card per qualifying receipt, link your Crazy Points account, in-game rewards expire 30 days after they load. The board shown above is a sample.

Purchase required. Bowl Games runs September 20 – November 14, 2026 at participating Missouri and Illinois locations. Play your own printed Crazy Bowls & Wraps receipt within 14 days of purchase; one play per order number. Your order decides which Huddle card a receipt earns, and not every receipt earns one. Rewards require a linked Crazy Points account, load the next day, expire 30 days after they load, and are limited to one of each per person per season. Collect all five Huddle cards and a regular bowl or wrap is on us; the set must be completed within the season. Taco Kit: weeks run Sunday through Saturday. The first kit is awarded at the Saturday, October 3, 2026 lock and weekly thereafter through November 14, 2026. At each participating store, the guest with the most verified receipt scans that week wins a Taco Kit, dinner for four to five; ties are broken by the earliest qualifying scan. Winners are decided by scan count, not by random draw, and are notified by email; one kit per person for the season. Full rules: crazybowlsandwraps.com/bowl-games.
