Here is the fix. The Build-Your-Own Taco Kit lands with twelve tortilla shells, beans, shredded lettuce, cheddar, chips and warm queso, and everybody at the table drafts their own. No compromise pizza. Nobody picking things off.

Twelve shells. A twelve-team league. We are not saying that was on purpose.
