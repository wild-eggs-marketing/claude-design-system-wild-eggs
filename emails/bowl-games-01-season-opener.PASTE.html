BOWL GAMES  ·   LOCATIONS  ·   MY ACCOUNT

Bowl Games is free to play with a printed paper Crazy Bowls & Wraps receipt from your own purchase at a participating Missouri or Illinois location, dine-in, drive-thru, online pickup, third-party delivery or catering, dated September 20, 2026 or later and no more than 14 days old. Not every receipt earns a card. One pull per receipt; each printed order number plays once. The card you get is decided by fixed rules from what is printed on the receipt. Rewards load as Crazy Points wallet items to the account you link in the game, after you opt in, the next day, and are good for 30 days; each reward is once per person for the season, tied to that one account, no cash value, not transferable. Chip Teriyaki is a computer-generated character with a synthetic voice. Receipt won't read? Email a photo with your first name to bowlgames@crazybowlsandwraps.com.
