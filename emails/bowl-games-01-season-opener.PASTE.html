BOWL GAMES  ·   LOCATIONS  ·   MY ACCOUNT

Short version: printed receipt, one card per qualifying receipt, link your Crazy Points account, rewards expire 30 days after they load.

Purchase required. Bowl Games runs September 20 – November 14, 2026 at participating Missouri and Illinois locations. Play your own printed Crazy Bowls & Wraps receipt within 14 days of purchase; one play per order number. Your order decides your card, and not every receipt earns one. Rewards require a linked Crazy Points account, load the next day, expire 30 days after they load, and are limited to one of each per person per season. Taco Kit: beginning September 27, 2026, the guest with the most verified receipt scans at each participating store that week wins a Taco Kit, dinner for four to five. Winners are decided by scan count, not by random draw, and are notified by email; one kit per person for the season. Full rules: crazybowlsandwraps.com/bowl-games.
